# 109 — photoelectric-threshold

**Domain:** physics
**Suggested interaction pattern:** `guess-then-reveal`

## Concept

Light below a threshold frequency ejects no electrons however bright it is, and above it the electron energy depends on frequency alone, not intensity.

## Why it benefits from interaction

Guessing what turning up the brightness will do, then watching nothing happen, is the experiment that killed the wave only picture.

## What the reader should be able to do afterward

Predict the stopping voltage for a given metal and wavelength, and explain why intensity does not appear in the answer.

## Assertions the selftest must make

- the maximum electron energy is h*f minus the work function, to 1e-12, at 50 frequencies
- below threshold the current is exactly zero at all intensities, checked at 40 intensities
- above threshold the current is proportional to intensity with correlation 1 to 1e-12
- the threshold wavelength matches hc over the work function for 5 published metal work functions to 0.1 nm
- the fitted slope of stopping voltage against frequency recovers Planck's constant to 4 significant figures

# 008 — supply-demand-tax-incidence

**Domain:** economics
**Suggested interaction pattern:** `drag-parameter`

## Concept

Who bears a tax depends on relative elasticity, not on who legally pays it.

## Why it benefits from interaction

Dragging the two elasticities and watching the burden split move, while the
statutory side stays fixed, is the whole lesson in one gesture.

## What the reader should be able to do afterward

Predict the burden split from elasticities and explain why the statutory side
is irrelevant.

## Assertions the selftest must make

- consumer burden share equals Es/(Es+Ed) to 1e-10
- burden split is identical whether the tax is levied on buyers or sellers
- deadweight loss equals half the tax times the quantity reduction
- tax revenue equals tax times post-tax quantity exactly
- perfectly inelastic demand puts 100% of the burden on consumers

# 056 — comparative-advantage-ppf

**Domain:** economics
**Suggested interaction pattern:** `drag-parameter`

## Concept

Two producers gain from trade whenever their opportunity costs differ, even when one is worse at making everything.

## Why it benefits from interaction

Dragging each producer's productivity and watching the joint production frontier kink, then flatten when the ratios coincide, makes opportunity cost the operative quantity rather than absolute skill.

## What the reader should be able to do afterward

Identify who should specialise in what from a productivity table, and state when trade brings no gain.

## Assertions the selftest must make

- the joint frontier is the concave upper envelope of both producers' frontiers, verified at 200 points
- specialisation by lower opportunity cost achieves the frontier exactly, at 60 seeded productivity pairs
- gains from trade are exactly zero when the two opportunity-cost ratios are equal, to 1e-12
- total output under specialisation is greater than or equal to any tested non-specialised split
- absolute advantage in both goods does not eliminate gains: at least one seeded case shows strict gain

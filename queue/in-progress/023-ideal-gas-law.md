# 023 — ideal-gas-law

**Domain:** chemistry
**Suggested interaction pattern:** `perturb-and-observe`

## Concept

Pressure, volume and temperature are locked together, and a real gas departs
from the ideal law predictably.

## Why it benefits from interaction

Holding one variable and pushing another, with the van der Waals curve
overlaid, shows exactly where ideality fails.

## What the reader should be able to do afterward

Predict a pressure change from a volume change and say when to reach for van
der Waals.

## Assertions the selftest must make

- PV = nRT holds to 1e-12 across the parameter grid
- one mole at STP occupies 22.414 L to 3 decimals
- Boyle: P1V1 equals P2V2 at fixed T to 1e-12
- van der Waals reduces to ideal as a and b approach zero
- the van der Waals critical point satisfies Tc = 8a/(27Rb)

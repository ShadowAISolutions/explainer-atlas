# 137 — tree-rings-and-climate

**Domain:** earth-science
**Suggested interaction pattern:** `time-scrubber`

## Concept

A tree ring is a year, and its width records that year's growing season, which is how climate is read centuries before anyone kept records.

## Why it benefits from interaction

Scrubbing along a core and matching its pattern to another core is cross dating, which is the whole method in one action.

## What the reader should be able to do afterward

Cross date two cores and say what a narrow ring does and does not tell you.

## Assertions the selftest must make

- cross correlation locates the true overlap of two synthetic cores exactly, on 300 seeded pairs
- the correlation at the true offset exceeds every other offset by a measured margin on 95 percent of seeded pairs
- detrending removes the age related growth curve, leaving a fitted slope within 1e-9 of zero
- the reconstructed climate signal correlates with the generating signal above 0.9 on noiseless data
- a missing ring shifts every earlier date by exactly one year, exhibited
